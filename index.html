Odd Entries

Odd Entries is an independent channel of short animated videos about real events from history that sound made up: hoaxes, strange wars, odd inventions and unsolved mysteries. Every video is researched from public sources and narrated over original animation.

Odd Entries Studio

Odd Entries Studio is our own desktop tool. We use it to upload the videos we make to our own accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook) and to read the public view counts of those videos. It is not offered to anyone else and does not access other people's accounts or data.
