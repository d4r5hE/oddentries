Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

This policy covers Odd Entries Studio, the desktop tool Odd Entries uses to publish its own videos, and this website.

Who uses the tool

Only Odd Entries. The tool signs in to Odd Entries' own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, with the permissions we grant it on each platform's own sign-in screen. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • Basic profile information of our own accounts (account name and ID), to confirm which account it is connected to.
  • Permission to upload our own videos to our own accounts (on TikTok, as drafts in our inbox that we review and post ourselves).
  • The public counts (views, likes, comments, shares) of our own videos, so we can see what works.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal information about viewers or any other users.
  • It does not show ads, sell data, or share data with anyone.

Storage and security

The sign-in tokens the platforms issue are stored only on our own computer and are never shared. Any of them can be revoked at any time in the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions).

Viewers

When you watch or interact with our videos on a platform, that platform's own privacy policy applies. We only see the totals the platform reports to us.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub's privacy statement applies to its servers.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will appear on this page. Questions: send a message to @oddentries on TikTok or Instagram.
